# Media jobs

Each asset carries **one** job in the effective loop. Medium is chosen for
that job, not because "we need to be on every platform".

| Job | Question the asset answers | Media that fit | Anti-fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mindshare** | When SCENE happens, do they think of NAME? | 6–15s loop, still with one distinctive object, audio sting, outdoor, pre-roll bumper | 3-minute manifesto, carousel of 12 features |
| **Clarity** | Can a stranger repeat problem + why you? | Screen recording, before/after still, 30–60s mechanism, landing hero | Mood film, founder origin, award reel |
| **Action** | What is the one next step, cheap to take? | Landing, reply-email, form, QR to a 2-min clip, pin comment | "Link in bio" with five destinations, 60-min webinar as first ask |
| **Retention** | After use, do they keep it and tell someone? | Customer clip with a number, Tuesday ritual content, community recap | Acquisition ads shown to existing users as if they were new |

## Multimedia plan rules

1. **Name the scene before the platform.** "Store manager, Sunday night,
   spreadsheet open" then pick IG / LINE / YouTube. Not "we need TikTok".
2. **One distinctive object** for mindshare (a color+object pair the brain
   can store). Not a new look per post.
3. **Sound-off first** for feeds; mechanism must still read as stills.
4. **Duration is a budget.** Default shorter. Justify every second past 15
   for mindshare, past 60 for clarity.
5. **Sequence, don't spray.** Campaign size: 1 mindshare object, 1 clarity
   piece, 1 action surface, 0–2 retention pieces. Extra posts must reuse
   those, not invent a second pitch.
6. **Owned > rented for action and retention.** Feeds can buy mindshare.
   The step and the kept promise live on a page, a thread, or a product
   surface you control.
7. **Language follows the person.** Write the unit in the audience's
   language. Chinese puff (`賦能`, `顛覆`, `信仰品牌`) fails the same lint
   as English puff.

## Delegation map

| Piece inside an asset | Hand to |
|---|---|
| Verbal unit (hook, VO, caption, hero, CTA) | `plain-sales-copy` |
| Look, type, motion, shot | `design-context` |
| Raster / video files if the user wants generation | `local-agent-pack` generate-* after the two briefs exist |
| "What should we even sell?" | stop → `interview-to-build` |
