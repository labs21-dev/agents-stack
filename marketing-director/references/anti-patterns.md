# Anti-patterns

## Self-toast campaign

Brief says "leading / 賦能 / future of work". Assets are a brand film with
no Friday-work and no number. KPI is "brand heat".

Fail: essence empty, effective loop empty, puff. Rewrite from friction +
delivery or stop.

## Fake Harley

Three-month-old SaaS asks to "become a lifestyle / 信仰品牌". Moodboard is
tattoos, queues, Christmas red.

Fail: level gate. Diagnose 1 or 2. Target at most 2. Harley is an exhibit
of residue, not a look.

## Awards as proof

Campaign case study leads with design prizes and like-counts. No mindshare
scene, no action, no retained user who can repeat the promise.

Fail: those are costs with applause. Effective loop is the scoreboard.

## Society sticker

Roster app launch themed 環保 / 性別平權 / 文明演進. None of the product
changes those.

Fail: `for_society` must be `omit`. Meaning-for-user is hours back.

## Push plan

Media plan is frequency + budget until conversion. No pull path, no
friction named.

Fail: hard rule 2. Name how the person with the pain meets the carrier.

## All-jobs film

One 90s film tries mindshare, spec, identity, CTA, founder why, and
society.

Fail: one job per asset. Split or cut.

## Puff waiver

"This is marketing, not a sales email, so adjectives are the point."

Fail: `plain-sales-copy` still owns the words. Soul is in the unsaid want
and the kept why, not in `革命性`.

## Product later

"We'll market it into existence; engineering will catch up."

Fail: delivery_check. If the promise cannot be kept this wave, kill the
wave. Marketing that outruns the product is a death accelerator.

## Platform first

"Give me 30 TikToks this month." No scene, no job mix, 30 second pitches.

Fail: size down to a campaign with 1+1+1+optional retention, then reuse.
Thirty posts without a distinctive object are noise.

## Passing shape (restaurant roster, campaign)

- Essence: Sunday-night spreadsheet; POS+LINE tap; "roster" in that scene;
  $20k/store already kept.
- Soul: 店長, fear of a wrong shift on LINE; why = no Friday spreadsheet.
- Meaning: for_business = roster R&D not unseen; for_user = 40h back;
  for_society = omit.
- Level: current 1–2, target 2. No faith claim.
- Effective: Sunday spreadsheet → this name; 40h / 5s / $20k; send a
  2-min tap; post-use the $20k still true.
- Media: 9s still+sting (mindshare), 40s POS-to-LINE screen recording
  (clarity), landing with the 2-min clip (action), one 店長 clip with the
  number (retention).
