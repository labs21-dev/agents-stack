# Five dimensions (fields, not essays)

Use these as a checklist the director fills. Do not write a philosophy
memo. Each dimension collapses to the fields below. Empty fields are
labeled assumptions or explicit omissions.

## 1. Essence — 本質

Marketing ≠ advertising. Marketing = insight + carrier + position + delivery.

```
friction: <who is in pain or want, doing what, how often>
carrier: <the product as the thing that holds the value, one sentence>
position: <the slot in the mind: when SCENE, they think of NAME>
delivery: <the promise the product already keeps, with a number>
pull: <how a person who has the pain finds this without a shove>
```

Fail if `carrier` is "our brand" or `delivery` is a future roadmap.

## 2. Soul — 靈魂

Two layers only. Both must be specific enough to film.

```
person: <role + one named life, not "users" or "traffic">
unsaid: <anxiety / vanity / fear / want they would not put on a form>
why: <one sentence the company already acts on when nobody is watching>
```

`why` is not a slogan. "We exist to empower" fails. "We refuse to ship
roster software that still needs a spreadsheet on Friday" can pass.

Nike/Apple are examples of *finished* souls, not templates to copy.

## 3. Meaning — 意義

Three audiences. Fill only what is true.

```
for_business: <what dies if this stays unseen — inventory of R&D, a cycle>
for_user: <the concrete after-state: hours back, money kept, a job done>
for_society: omit | <only if the product actually spreads a practice>
```

`for_user` is not "a better version of myself" as a phrase. Write the
after-state in numbers and Tuesday behavior.

`for_society` defaults to `omit`. Phones, public health, and standards
can fill it. A B2B roster app does not get 文明演進.

## 4. End — 盡頭

The end-state is culture and default. It is a diagnosis of *now*, not a
Q3 objective. Fields live in `references/level-gate.md`.

```
current_level: 1 | 2 | 3 | 4
target_level: 1 | 2 | 3 | 4
evidence: <what already happens in the wild>
gap: <what would have to be true in the product and the community>
```

"Become air and water" / 不銷而銷 is level 4. Campaigns do not produce it.

## 5. Effective — 有效

The only closed loop that means the work worked. Not likes. Not design
awards. Not "the room liked the cut".

```
mindshare: <SCENE → first reflex NAME>
clarity: <one sentence a 12-year-old can repeat: problem + why you>
action: <the one behavior this wave is for: click / reply / buy / forward>
delivery_check: <post-use proof the product exceeds the line we just drew>
compound: <how a kept promise turns the user into the next marketer>
```

Fail closed:

- KPIs are impressions, likes, awards, "brand heat" with no action
- `clarity` needs a glossary
- `action` is "go viral"
- `delivery_check` is "we'll figure out the product later"

If `delivery_check` cannot be kept, the director kills the campaign.
Marketing that outruns the product is a death accelerator.
