# Anti-patterns

## Failed unit — adjectives, length, needs a glossary

> We are a leading AI-driven big-data analytics company. We provide a
> revolutionary end-to-end solution that uses powerful patented algorithms
> to give enterprises extremely smooth and comprehensive digital-transformation
> enablement, substantially lifting operational synergy.

Heard: "so what do you do?"

Fails: puff list, we-center, no pain, no cost, no number, no CTA, grandma fail.

Chinese twin of the same failure:

> 我們是一家領先的 AI 驅動大數據分析公司。我們提供革命性的端到端解決方案，
> 利用強大的專利演算法，為企業提供極致流暢且全方位的數位轉型賦能，大幅提升
> 運營綜效。

## Passing unit — 4 sentences, nouns/verbs/numbers

> Most chain restaurants spend a store manager 40 hours a month reconciling
> and rostering by hand. That is time they are not on the floor, and about
> $20k of management cost per store per month. The software connects the POS
> to staff LINE; one tap builds the roster and payroll in 5 seconds. Last month
> [named chain] went live and each store cut that $20k. If Wednesday afternoon
> is free, I send a 2-minute recording of the tap?

Heard: "how does the LINE connection work?" / "can you send it?" — curiosity,
not a glossary request.

## Common failure modes

| Mode | What it looks like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Self-toast | company adjectives, awards, "patented" | Delete. Put a named customer and a number. |
| Feature dump | 8 bullets of modules | One mechanism sentence. Move the rest to a later unit, still linted. |
| Vision pitch | "the future of work" | Replace with last-night's work and this month's cost. |
| Close-as-CTA | "book a 60-min demo" | 2-minute artifact or their own numbers. |
| We-memoir | "we founded / we believe / our mission" | Start at *they* do X by hand. |
| Fake proof | "leading brands trust us" | Name + date + number, or drop proof and label the assumption. |
| AI spice | "powered by our LLM pipeline" | Say the visible step the buyer sees. |
| Two products | unit sells A, then mentions B | One product per unit. |
| Glossary footnote | pitch + "POS = point of sale" | If they need the footnote, the noun was wrong. |

## Contrast (required, not decorative)

The unit must contain a gap the brain can feel:

- hand process vs one tap
- hours vs seconds
- money leaving this month vs money that stays

If both sides of the gap are "our old way vs our new way", that is still a
we-memoir. The gap is *their* today vs *their* Tuesday after the tap.
